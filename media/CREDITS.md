# Credits — the six Möbius plates

Each plate is a **generated image**, not a photograph. The geometry comes from a
3D scene and a depth map; the surface material and the ground are described in
text; projective geometry verifies the result. Every plate was measured — the
bird's height in pixels, its position in the frame, the scale of the ground —
and where measurement and eye disagreed, the eye decided, with the number kept
on record beside it.

## The banner

The header image of this profile is built from the **Pampa** plate — the one
that set the scale target for all six, kept in its first version, untouched. It
is therefore a derivative of the same ground reference, and the attribution
above covers it: *Dyckia remotiflora*, rafa perazzolo, CC BY,
[iNaturalist 329617487](https://www.inaturalist.org/observations/329617487).

## Species depicted

Scarlet macaw (*Ara macao*) · red-legged seriema (*Cariama cristata*) · caatinga
parakeet (*Eupsittula cactorum*) · toco toucan (*Ramphastos toco*) · rufous
hornero (*Furnarius rufus*) · jabiru stork (*Jabiru mycteria*).

Standing heights used as the scale ruler were taken from published sources; two
of the six are published as standing height, and the other four are derived from
total length and marked as estimates in the working notes — because in
long-tailed species more than half the published length is tail.
